Basic English for simple communication.

Family – parents, siblings, and relatives
Food – things we eat and drink
Weather – sunny, rainy, cloudy, etc.

📙 PET (B1)

Intermediate vocabulary for daily life.

Environment – the natural world around us
Education – learning and schooling
Health – physical and mental well-being

📕 FCE (B2)

Upper-intermediate vocabulary for work and study.

Globalization – worldwide integration
Sustainable – able to be maintained
Innovation – new ideas and methods

📓 CAE (C1)

Advanced vocabulary for professional use.

Meticulous – very careful and precise
Phenomenon – a remarkable event
Exemplary – serving as a perfect example

Academic vocabulary for university and migration.

Hypothesis – a proposed explanation
Implications – possible consequences
